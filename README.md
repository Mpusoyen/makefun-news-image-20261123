A dark "global briefing board" summing up Monday, 23 November 2026: a dot-matrix world map on deep navy with six amber pins placed where the day's stories happened — the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya with no deal, Democrats clinching 258 US House seats (their biggest majority since 1992) in Washington, Nepal's prime minister meeting Narendra Modi in Delhi, the search resuming after the deadly candy-factory fire in Banten, Indonesia, the Russian cargo ship Sparta II still burning off Finland, and "Sunrise on the Reaping" topping the North American box office from Los Angeles. A numbered panel below the map tells each story, keyed to its pin. The poster is rendered from `poster.html` with headless Chromium and shipped as `entry.png` (1024×1024, deterministic — two renders are byte-identical).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. No network is used.
